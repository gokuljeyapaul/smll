# Travel Policy 2026

## Limits

Reimbursement for travel expenses is capped at $500 per trip.

The standard domestic per diem is $65 per day.

## Flights and cars

All flights must be booked in economy class.

Rental cars are reimbursed only for the compact category.

## Approvals and deadlines

Travel bookings require approval from the employee's direct manager.

Travel expense claims must be submitted within 30 days of returning.

Business travel is booked through the Cornerstone travel platform.

Same-day travel bookings require a director's approval in the booking tool.
