# Holiday Schedule

The company observes 11 public holidays per year.

The office is closed between Christmas and New Year's Day.

## Working practices

Pallets with cracked boards are removed from circulation and taken to the timber skip. Racking teams flag any pallet that looks warped or water-damaged.

Outbound pallets are wrapped with film until the load is stable and nothing overhangs. Loose cartons are re-stacked before wrapping starts.

Every forklift gets a walkaround inspection at the start of each shift. Operators log the check even when nothing looks wrong.

Forklift operators slow to a crawl near pedestrian walkways and blind corners. Eye contact with a pedestrian always yields the right of way.

The pick zone floor markings separate pedestrian lanes from machine lanes. Repainting the lanes is part of the annual maintenance round.

Freight surcharges on customer invoices use the standard surcharge coding list. Anything outside the list needs a supervisor note on the invoice.

Cross-border loads need their customs paperwork pre-checked the day before dispatch. Missing stamps are escalated to the export desk immediately.

Drivers complete a daily walkaround of lights, tires, and reflectors before leaving the yard. Defects are written in the vehicle book and reported at handover.

Loads are secured with straps and edge protectors before the trailer doors close. The strap count depends on the load, not on the schedule.

Cold-chain temperature logs are reviewed by the shift supervisor each morning. Excursions outside the band are annotated with a cause and a fix.

Returns are unpacked, inspected, and either restocked or marked for disposal. The returns bench keeps separate totes for each outcome.

Damage discovered at unload is photographed and logged the same day. Claims are assembled from the photos plus the delivery confirmation scan.

Delivery confirmations are scanned into the system as soon as the signature is collected. Paper manifests are boxed for the weekly audit sample.

Route plans are reviewed for efficiency at the weekly dispatch meeting. Recurring detours are written up as candidate route changes.

Fuel cards are only used for assigned vehicles, and receipts are kept for reconciliation. Personal purchases on a fuel card go to People Operations.

Yard drivers keep to a walking pace between the dock doors and the trailer park. The yard lights are checked as part of the evening shift close-out.

Forklift batteries rotate through the charging station so a spare is always ready. Charging bays stay clear of packaging waste and loose film.

Weekly stock counts are split between the warehouse teams so counts stay fresh. Blind recounts settle any discrepancy above tolerance before posting.
