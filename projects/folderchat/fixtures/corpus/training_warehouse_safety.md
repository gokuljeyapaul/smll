# Warehouse Safety Training

Warehouse safety training is mandatory for all staff within their first 2 weeks.

## Operational notes

Route plans are reviewed for efficiency at the weekly dispatch meeting. Recurring detours are written up as candidate route changes.

Fuel cards are only used for assigned vehicles, and receipts are kept for reconciliation. Personal purchases on a fuel card go to People Operations.

Yard drivers keep to a walking pace between the dock doors and the trailer park. The yard lights are checked as part of the evening shift close-out.

Forklift batteries rotate through the charging station so a spare is always ready. Charging bays stay clear of packaging waste and loose film.

Weekly stock counts are split between the warehouse teams so counts stay fresh. Blind recounts settle any discrepancy above tolerance before posting.

Spill kits are stocked at the loading bays and checked as part of the safety rounds. Used absorbents are logged and replaced the same week.

Chemical handling requires the gloves and goggles from the PPE station. The safety data sheets binder lives next to the decanting bench.

Manual lifting reminders are posted at the inbound docks: bend the knees, keep the load close, and ask for help with awkward shapes.

Ladders used for racking access carry a current inspection tag. Ladders without a tag are taken out of service until re-inspected.

Emergency drills are scheduled throughout the year, and participation is recorded. Drill notes go into the safety folder after each run.

The kitchenette cleaning rota is pinned inside the cabinet door. Sign-offs go on the rota sheet at the end of each week.

Whiteboards are photographed before being wiped so notes are not lost. Photos are attached to the meeting record in the team folder.

Cardboard goes in the gray bins, and stretch wrap goes in the clear bags. Full bins are taken to the dock-side collection point at shift end.

Quiet hours in the open office keep calls in the phone booths. Larger reviews move to the booked meeting rooms.

Cable trays under the desks are tidied whenever a desk is reassigned. Label printers and spare labels live in the stationery drawer.

Trailer seals are checked against the manifest before departure. Seal numbers are copied onto the handover sheet by the departing driver.

Dock doors are kept closed between arrivals to keep the loading bay warm. The dock schedule whiteboard is updated as arrivals are confirmed.

The shift log records handover notes, open issues, and anything the next shift should watch. The log is countersigned at the start of each shift.
