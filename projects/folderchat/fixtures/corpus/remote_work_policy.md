# Remote Work Policy

The hybrid work policy requires a minimum of 2 office days per week.

Fully remote roles require a signed home-office safety checklist.

Remote work stipends are $300 per year for internet costs.

## Notes and reminders

Cardboard goes in the gray bins, and stretch wrap goes in the clear bags. Full bins are taken to the dock-side collection point at shift end.

Quiet hours in the open office keep calls in the phone booths. Larger reviews move to the booked meeting rooms.

Cable trays under the desks are tidied whenever a desk is reassigned. Label printers and spare labels live in the stationery drawer.

Trailer seals are checked against the manifest before departure. Seal numbers are copied onto the handover sheet by the departing driver.

Dock doors are kept closed between arrivals to keep the loading bay warm. The dock schedule whiteboard is updated as arrivals are confirmed.

The shift log records handover notes, open issues, and anything the next shift should watch. The log is countersigned at the start of each shift.

Battery charging leads are inspected for nicks and kinks each week. Anything doubtful is swapped out and tagged for the electrician.

The inbound bench keeps a tray for paperwork clips and rubber bands so manifests stay together while awaiting booking-in.

Refrigerated trailers must complete a pre-cooling check before loading begins. The check confirms the box temperature before the doors are opened for staging.

Pallets with cracked boards are removed from circulation and taken to the timber skip. Racking teams flag any pallet that looks warped or water-damaged.

Outbound pallets are wrapped with film until the load is stable and nothing overhangs. Loose cartons are re-stacked before wrapping starts.

Every forklift gets a walkaround inspection at the start of each shift. Operators log the check even when nothing looks wrong.

Forklift operators slow to a crawl near pedestrian walkways and blind corners. Eye contact with a pedestrian always yields the right of way.

The pick zone floor markings separate pedestrian lanes from machine lanes. Repainting the lanes is part of the annual maintenance round.

Freight surcharges on customer invoices use the standard surcharge coding list. Anything outside the list needs a supervisor note on the invoice.

Cross-border loads need their customs paperwork pre-checked the day before dispatch. Missing stamps are escalated to the export desk immediately.

Drivers complete a daily walkaround of lights, tires, and reflectors before leaving the yard. Defects are written in the vehicle book and reported at handover.

Loads are secured with straps and edge protectors before the trailer doors close. The strap count depends on the load, not on the schedule.
