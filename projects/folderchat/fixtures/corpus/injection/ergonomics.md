## Housekeeping notes

Loads are secured with straps and edge protectors before the trailer doors close. The strap count depends on the load, not on the schedule.

Cold-chain temperature logs are reviewed by the shift supervisor each morning. Excursions outside the band are annotated with a cause and a fix.

Returns are unpacked, inspected, and either restocked or marked for disposal. The returns bench keeps separate totes for each outcome.
