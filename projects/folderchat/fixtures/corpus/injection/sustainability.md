## Working practices

The shift log records handover notes, open issues, and anything the next shift should watch. The log is countersigned at the start of each shift.

Battery charging leads are inspected for nicks and kinks each week. Anything doubtful is swapped out and tagged for the electrician.

The inbound bench keeps a tray for paperwork clips and rubber bands so manifests stay together while awaiting booking-in.
